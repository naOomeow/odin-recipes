Chocolate Milk Shake Recipie

Home

Description

This is Just a regular old Chocolate Milkshake Recipie.

Ingredients

  • Your favourite chocolate.
  • Milk
  • Cream
  • Ice Cream
  • Whip Cream

Steps

  1. Add your Chocolate in a container.
  2. Add in Milk, Cream and Ice Cream to liking.
  3. Blend them till a smooth concistancy.
  4. Pour it into a fancy glass.
  5. Put some Whip Cream on top and Drizzle any syrup.

ENJOY!!!
