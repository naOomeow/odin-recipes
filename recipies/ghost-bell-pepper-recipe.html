Ghost Bell Pepper Recipie

HOME

Description (for one serving)

  • 1 Bell pepper
  • 1/2 Ghost
  • A table spoon of Chilli flakes
  • A tea spoon of Vinegar

Steps

  1. Wash your Bell Pepper
  2. Cut out the stem on top with the seeds
  3. Remove the seeds
  4. Mix your vinegar and chilli flakes
  5. Put them in the Bell Pepper
  6. Put the stem part back on and shake it a bit
  7. Bake/Microwave it for TWO minutes
