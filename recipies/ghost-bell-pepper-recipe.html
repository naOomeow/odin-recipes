Ghost Bell Pepper Recipie

HOME

Description (for one serving)

Here is a fun halloween recipie to make Bell Peppers with Ghosts These Have a slight tangy ghosty taste

Ingredients


  • 1 Bell pepper
  • 1/2 Ghost
  • A table spoon of Chilli flakes
  • A tea spoon of Vinegar

Steps


  1. Wash your Bell Pepper
  2. Cut out the stem on top with the seeds
  3. Remove the seeds
  4. Mix your vinegar and chilli flakes
  5. Put them in the Bell Pepper
  6. Add your ghost in Be careful with the handling
  7. Put the stem part back on and shake it a bit
  8. Bake/Microwave it for TWO minutes

ENJOY!!!
